01 — About

About Me

My tech journey has been diverse and highly driven by hands-on creation. I started my path by diving into hardware, learning IoT, coordinating interactive workshops, and personally training hundreds of students in the fundamentals of connected technologies.

My fascination with interactive experiences eventually led me into game development and 3D modeling using Unity, Blender, and Maya. After graduation, I put these skills to work through freelancing and internships, developing several immersive AR/VR applications—including a specialized educational app mapping human organ anatomy. During this period of growth, I also expanded my expertise into the realms of cybersecurity and cloud architecture, studying Oracle Cloud and Azure.

This broad technical foundation ultimately led me to LTIMindtree, where I found my true niche as a focused and enthusiastic Identity and Access Management (IAM) Professional. Today, I specialize in navigating complex security landscapes, working extensively with SailPoint IdentityIQ, Windows Server, Active Directory, LDAP, Unix access protocols, and ServiceNow testing to build robust, secure access solutions for critical enterprise assets.
