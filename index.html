01 — About

About Me

My tech journey has always been driven by curiosity, hands-on learning, and the desire to build things that solve real problems. I started by exploring hardware and IoT, coordinating interactive workshops, and training hundreds of students in the fundamentals of connected technologies.

My interest in interactive technology later led me into game development and 3D modeling using Unity, Blender, and Maya. Through freelancing and internships, I worked on immersive AR/VR applications, including an educational application focused on human anatomy. Along the way, I expanded my knowledge into cybersecurity and cloud technologies, including Oracle Cloud and Azure.

This diverse technical foundation eventually led me to LTIMindtree, where I found my professional focus in Identity and Access Management (IAM). Today, my experience centers around SailPoint IdentityIQ, Windows Server, Active Directory, LDAP, Unix access environments, and ServiceNow, with a focus on identity governance, access provisioning, access workflows, and secure enterprise access.

What I enjoy most about IAM is solving the balance between security, accessibility, and usability — ensuring that the right users receive the right access at the right time, while that access remains governed, reviewable, and secure.

I learn best by building, testing, troubleshooting, and understanding how systems work end to end. My background across engineering, creative technology, cloud, and cybersecurity has shaped the way I approach problems today: with curiosity, adaptability, and a strong hands-on mindset.

My current direction is to deepen my expertise in identity security, cloud identity, automation, identity protocols, Zero Trust, and security architecture, while continuing to build practical solutions that make enterprise identity and access more secure, efficient, and manageable.
